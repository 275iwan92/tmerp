How the bin is suggested (engine suggests, officer confirms)
  1. Zone matches the item class and the area Mixing rule (1 item per bin, mixed items, or 1 batch per bin).
  2. Grain direction and certification (FSC / food grade) match the zone.
  3. Capacity: weight, pallet count, and stack limit (max stack).
  4. Pick order: FEFO zones keep the earliest expiry nearest the door.
  5. Officer scans the pallet, then the bin; a different bin needs a reason.
