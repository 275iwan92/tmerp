How drag and drop works

1 Drag
Pick a bar and move it along the row (time) or to another machine of the same process (row). Only machines certified for the process accept it.
2 Preview
A green ghost shows where it lands. The impact box lists new dates, setup change and jobs that become late, before anything is saved.
3 Rules
Locked window, blocked gates, and a machine that is down refuse the drop (red). Breaking the lock needs Hot job and a reason.
4 Draft, then Confirm
A move becomes part of the Draft. The officer presses Confirm plan; nothing changes on the shop floor before that.
