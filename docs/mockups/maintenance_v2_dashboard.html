10Formulas (same as the engines in the code)
IndicatorFormulaSource of data
OEEAvailability × Performance × Quality. If any factor cannot be calculated, OEE shows "not calculated", never 0 %.Production engine (read only here)
Availability (OEE)(Planned minutes − Unplanned downtime) ÷ Planned minutes. Planned minutes = working days × shifts per day × hours per shift × 60, with 6 working days per week.Machine calendar and downtime log
PerformanceGood output ÷ (Run hours × Target speed per hour), at most 100 %.Production operations and output
QualityGood output ÷ (Good output + Spoilage + Waste).Production output
MTTRTotal breakdown downtime hours ÷ number of breakdowns.Downtime log, kind BREAKDOWN
MTBF(Period hours − breakdown downtime hours) ÷ number of breakdowns.Downtime log, kind BREAKDOWN
Inherent availabilityMTBF ÷ (MTBF + MTTR). Shown in Machine Reliability, not the same as Availability in OEE.Calculated from MTBF and MTTR
PM CompliancePMO done by due date plus tolerance ÷ PMO due in the period.Preventive Maintenance Orders
Planned workOrders that are not BREAKDOWN ÷ all orders in the period.Maintenance Orders
Backlog (weeks)Estimated hours of open orders ÷ technician hours available per week.Open orders and technician roster
Maintenance costSpare parts + technician hours × rate + vendor service.Order parts, labor, and vendor lines
Legenda
1
Enam indikator dengan target. Target diketik user di Settings › Constants. Lampu hijau, kuning, merah terhadap target. Klik membuka laporan Machine Reliability atau PM Compliance.
2
Angka tindakan. Hal yang perlu dikerjakan hari ini. Klik membuka daftarnya.
3
Tren 12 bulan. Melihat apakah kepatuhan PM membaik atau memburuk.
4
Mesin dengan downtime terbanyak. Dasar memutuskan perbaikan, improvement, atau penggantian.
5
Kerusakan per komponen. Pareto dari kode Component. Kerusakan berulang memicu saran Improvement.
6
OEE hanya ditampilkan. Dihitung oleh modul Production. Maintenance menyumbang Availability.
7
Biaya. Part, tenaga teknisi, dan jasa vendor dari Maintenance Order.
8
Laporan Maintenance KPI berisi grafik dan rumus. Dibuka dari kartu di Dashboard. Satu laporan kaya kolom, bukan banyak laporan kecil.
9
Grafik rugi OEE. Dari 100 % waktu terencana, berapa hilang karena ketersediaan, kecepatan, dan mutu.
10
Rumus sama dengan kode. OEE, MTBF, dan MTTR memakai perhitungan Production yang sudah ada. Maintenance tidak menghitung ulang dengan cara berbeda.
