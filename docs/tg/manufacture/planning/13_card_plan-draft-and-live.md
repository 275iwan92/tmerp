# Concept card: Plan, Draft and Live Schedule

> Written 2026-10-17 (Manufacture group). "FACT" = seen first-hand in this session: mockup DB (`--env=mockup`, `tm2*` = `ddt*`; counts = `count(*)` today), code with line numbers, and read-only calls of `Ppic::live()`, `Ppic::gantt()`, `Ppic::stats()`, `Ppic::exceptions()`. "FROM DOCUMENT" = not checked.

- **Document type**: concept card. **Module/sub-module**: Manufacture > Planning > PPIC Workspace (`PROD_MPS`, `v2:production-scheduling`), Live Schedule (`PROD_LIVE`, `v2:production-live`), Colour Sequence (`PROD_COLOR_SEQ`, `v2:ppic-color-sequence`), Work Order form (Simulation, QUE).
- **Canonical name**: Schedule = the placement columns of `tm2prd.prd_wo_operation` in three layers: **Draft** (the proposal: `draft_machine_id`, `draft_start`, `draft_finish` with `plan_status = DRAFT`), **Live** (the schedule in force: `machine_id`, `target_start`, `target_finish` with `plan_status = CONFIRMED`) and **Simulation** (what the Work Order form shows: the whole schedule in force plus Drafts, other WOs greyed). The runs and decisions are in `prd_plan_run` and `prd_plan_decision`.
- **Synonyms**: plan, Draft plan, APPLY TO LIVE SCHEDULE, Confirmed, MPS (old name), Gantt, Kanban, Calendar, Grid, Monitoring, simulation layer.
- **NOT the same as**: the old backward schedule `target_start` written at WO creation, the CPDEL customer slot (`../../sales/order/21_engine_cpdel-promise.md`), the shift roster (`../ppic/12_card_shift-and-calendar.md`).

## Findings (recorded, not fixed)
1. **Live Gantt is empty while Live Schedule lists 13 operations.** `Ppic::gantt` removes operations whose finish is before the origin (yesterday 00:00); an operation that is late by more than a day disappears from the picture although it is still in the table as "Late". The automated test `tests/Feature/LiveScheduleTest.php` fails today on this (`assertCount(13, gantt tasks)`, 0 found; run with `--env=mockup`). FACT (test run and code).
2. **Dummy Live data is stale**: the 13 CONFIRMED operations date from 13/14 October and nobody has stopped them; 2 `RUN` timer segments are still open. Not a code error; it makes the Live Schedule and "Behind plan" look wrong. FACT (query).
3. **No scheduled run was ever recorded** (DAILY 0, SHIFT 0); `23_engine_auto-plan-runner.md` finding 2. FACT.
4. **The Live view is a screen of `prd_wo_operation`, not of a separate live table**: after the next planner run, unlocked CONFIRMED operations turn back to DRAFT and drop out of Live Schedule (the filter is `plan_status = CONFIRMED` or RUNNING). WO2600273 shows 3 operations in Live and 5 not. FACT (query, code).
5. **Two sets of dates on one operation**: `target_start/finish` (confirmed, also written by the legacy backward scheduler at WO creation) and `draft_*`; a reader must follow `plan_status` to know which one is real (`Ppic::operations` does; other readers such as the old board and `RateBandResolver` read `target_*` directly). FACT (grep).
6. **Planned finish is not used by Sales**: the customer slot and `ready_at` of CPDEL are managed by hand (`../../sales/order/21_engine_cpdel-promise.md`); the planner's forecast finish and its `LATE_RISK` are not sent there. FACT (grep of readers; `Cpdel` has no read of `prd_wo_operation`).
